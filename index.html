PaperLens is a small browser agent inspired by Robert S. Laramee’s framework for extracting the essentials from a research paper. Upload a PDF, let DeepSeek structure it, then export an editable deck or an animated video.

Why this structure?

Bob’s filter, turned into an agent.

Laramee’s method says the useful output of reading a paper is a small set of essentials: the concept, its implementation, the previous work it builds upon, the data characteristics, the visualization techniques, and the application domain. PaperLens makes those six slots the model’s contract.
